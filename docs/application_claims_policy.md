# Application claims policy

## Principle
Every claim in a CV, cover letter, recruiter message, form answer or interview-preparation artifact must be accurate, supportable and proportionate.

## Allowed claims
- Duties and tools recorded in the configured `user_profile.yml`.
- Education, certifications and projects with documented evidence.
- A role-specific explanation connecting existing work to a vacancy.

## Disallowed claims
- Invented production ownership, leadership, years of experience, KPIs, client names, cloud deployments, language levels, visa status, availability, salary expectations or security clearance.
- Copying a job requirement into a CV when it is not evidenced.
- Saying a project was built independently when it was collaborative or AI-assisted.

## AI-assisted work
AI may help draft, explain, refactor or test. Candidate profile context and job-description content may be sent to configured external AI providers for matching or generation; the candidate remains responsible for reviewing code and text. Do not upload confidential employer data, credentials, private logs or unpublished code to AI systems unless the provider and use are explicitly approved.

## Human gate
No application may be sent automatically. A human must review the selected profile, all placeholders, job requirements, generated CV, cover letter and form responses before submission.

## Audit trail
Store the job URL, job snapshot, selected target profile, score explanation, final documents and date of human approval in the CRM/application record.
